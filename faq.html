Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our food and services.

What are your delivery hours and areas?

We deliver from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Sunday. We currently deliver to all areas within Kampala and the surrounding suburbs.

Do you charge a delivery fee?

No, we provide 100% free delivery within the local Kampala area for all orders above 10,000 UGX. For orders below this amount, a small fee of 2,000 UGX applies.

Do you cater for large weddings and events?

Yes! Our "Event" package can be customized for weddings, parties, and corporate events. We can cater for groups of 50 to 500 people. Please contact us at least 3 days in advance to discuss your menu.

Can I mix and match the package items?

Absolutely. You can swap pizzas for extra rolex or kikomando at no extra charge. We want to make sure you get exactly what you and your guests will enjoy.

How do I pay for my order?

Currently, we accept cash on delivery and Mobile Money (MTN MoMo and Airtel Money). You can also pay via the WhatsApp link when you confirm your order.
